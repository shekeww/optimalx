07 / Arabic-First Website
Arabic is the primary product experience
The website launches in Arabic first. The Arabic experience is designed independently for Saudi customers; English is a secondary language layer.

Default language

Arabic / Saudi market. Homepage, navigation, search guidance, product information, filters, account, cart, policies and transactional support are Arabic by default.

Secondary language

English remains available through a clear language switch. English copy mirrors the approved Arabic meaning rather than becoming the content source.

Navigation
الرئيسية · تسوّق حسب الهدف · الفئات · العروض

Short, familiar labels. Avoid literal translations that feel unnatural.

Search
ابحث عن منتج أو هدف

Search prompts should describe both product and intent.

Primary CTA
اكتشف حسب هدفك

Actions are clear and outcome-led.

Arabic UX implementation rules

  • RTL layout is the default state, not a mirrored afterthought.
  • Cairo Bold for titles and key numbers; Cairo Regular for body and UI copy.
  • Arabic line lengths, button widths and card density are tuned independently from English.
  • Product names, ingredients and factual specifications remain exact and easy to scan.
  • Use Arabic numerals and Saudi-relevant currency formatting where supported by Salla.
  • Do not mix Arabic and English inside one sentence unless the product or brand term requires it.
  • WhatsApp, delivery, payment and returns information should be understandable without opening a second language layer.
  • Campaign Arabic may be warmer than the UI, but it must remain credible and brand-consistent.
Content rule Arabic is written first, reviewed for Saudi naturalness, then localized into English. Never translate the English page back into Arabic as the primary workflow.

Rule 01Use Salla-maintained components, localization and native commerce capabilities before creating duplicates.

25 / Acceptance Criteria
The quality bar

Brand

  • Identity feels premium, practical and Saudi-aware.
  • Voice is consistent across website and social.
  • Arabic is natural, not literal translation.
  • Seasonal accents do not break the master identity.

UX

  • Every major page has one clear job.
  • Customers can start from a goal or product.
  • Search and filters are usable on mobile.
  • PDP answers pre-purchase questions.

Technical

  • Predefined Salla page paths remain intact.
  • Reusable components are used consistently.
  • Custom JS is minimal and purposeful.
  • Checkout remains Salla-native.

Social

  • Content maps to real landing pages.
  • Every post has a clear job and CTA.
  • Visual language is recognizably OptimalX.
  • Trust and education are not replaced by discounting.

Services

  • Services are a first-class navigation branch, not buried in content.
  • Certified expert profiles include credentials, scope, service details and booking entry points.
  • Booking products work through native Salla scheduling where applicable.
  • Service-to-product and product-to-service journeys are coherent.
  • Medical/health language is reviewed against the practitioner’s actual permitted scope.
  • Saudi Arabic copy receives native-speaker review before launch.
The final experience should feel like a modern Saudi health-commerce platform — not a template store with a new logo.

Arabic / Saudi

  • Arabic is the default locale and RTL is correct across all V1 templates.
  • Arabic copy reads naturally for Saudi customers and is not a literal translation of English.
  • Primary navigation, search, product information, checkout entry points and support content are complete in Arabic.
  • Cairo typography is applied consistently to Arabic UI and content.
