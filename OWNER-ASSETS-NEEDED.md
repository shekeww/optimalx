**How to take them with your phone**

- A plain white background, or the pack on a white sheet of paper.
- Daylight by a window or a bright lamp, no flash.
- Straight on, the front facing the camera (lay bars and bags flat and shoot from above).
- The whole pack in the frame, with a little white space around it.
- The label sharp and readable. No filters, no edits.
